3. Methodology and limitations for panel defense

Model design

Random Forest Regression with 300 trees, leaf size of 4 and inputs weekday, month and slot index. The model was trained on 195 simulated weekdays (1,560 slots) from January–September 2026 and evaluated on the later October–December 2026 dates (66 days, 528 slots).

Graph interpretation

When the predicted and simulated actual lines are close, errors are small. A positive residual means the model underestimated. A negative residual means it overestimated. Trend comparison and residual distribution are the primary evaluation evidence.

Observed limitations

Even though the model slightly improves upon the training-only slot-median baseline, the improvement is modest. Both training and test data came from the same simulation process, so results cannot validate predictions on an independent branch.

Because verified arrival timestamps are unavailable, these are slot-level teller-queue count estimates, not verified hourly customer arrivals. Real timestamped bank-arrival data are required to establish the full operational objective.

This page is an isolated research demonstration. It does not alter the bank's database, appointment workflow, admin credentials, customer scheduling, or model training. Validation data originate from the installed model's local regression_holdout.csv.
