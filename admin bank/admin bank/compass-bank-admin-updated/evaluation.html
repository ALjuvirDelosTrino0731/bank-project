Important limitations

  • Three arrival-time records are invalid and excluded; three daily totals differ by one from the published counts.
  • Numeric H.MM conversions still require source-format verification.
  • Observations are grouped by bank, week, weekday and hour; exact calendar dates are unavailable.
  • The study describes customers served; arrivals and served customers are not necessarily identical.
  • 60 bank-days are a small forecasting sample. Predictions are not validated for the Philippine branch.

Defense wording: “We evaluated both models on a held-out observation week using published real banking observations. The results demonstrate the algorithms' operation, but do not establish reliable calendar-date predictions for our selected Philippine branch.”
