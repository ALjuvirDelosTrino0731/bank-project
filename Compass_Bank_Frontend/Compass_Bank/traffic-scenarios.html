How these estimates are produced

The daily Low / Moderate / High category comes from Random Forest Classification. The hourly customer counts come from Random Forest Regression. Week and month totals sum repeated weekday scenario estimates; they are not independently validated predictions. Weekends are shown as unavailable, not assumed to have zero visitors. The original observation weeks have no verified calendar dates, so no date, holiday, or seasonal features are used.

Held-out evaluation: classification accuracy 40% on 15 bank-days. This is exploratory research and should not be used for operational decisions without branch-specific validation.
